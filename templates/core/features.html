Organise

Nested colour-coded folders, free-form tags, ten categories, favourites and archive. Full-text style search across titles, issuers, reference numbers and tags, with filters for expiry, sensitivity and status, plus bulk actions.

Stay ahead

Set expiry dates and get notifications before renewal deadlines. The vault health score tells you what to fix first, flags duplicates and lists essential documents you have not filed yet.

Share safely

Links can expire, be capped by download count, and be password protected. Sensitive documents ask for your password again before download; critical ones can never be shared by public link.

Prove authenticity

Publish a verification certificate for any document. Recipients drop the file on the verification page and the fingerprint is compared in their own browser.
