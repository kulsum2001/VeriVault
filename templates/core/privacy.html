Privacy

This is a self-hosted application. The operator of this installation controls the data.

What is stored

Your account details, the documents and metadata you upload, and an audit log of actions taken in your vault (including IP address and browser user agent).

What is shared

Nothing leaves the server. There are no analytics, ad networks or third-party API calls. A verification certificate shows only a document's title, your display name, fingerprint, size, version and certification date.

Your controls

You can export every document as a ZIP, delete individual documents permanently, or delete your account and all associated files from the Profile page.
